A reversible discriminator left Witch Dock's existing renderer, atlas size, mask/AAID bindings, and allocation path intact and changed only the two assets' displayed normal-map bindings from 128² to their verified 512² resources. This accepts the 128→512 normal-map binding as the smallest causal difference for these comparisons and rejects `pixelsPerUnit`, larger atlas allocation, output sizing, or mask/AAID promotion as independently necessary for the observed gain.
